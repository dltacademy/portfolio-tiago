How I work

The DFM method applied to real projects: zero black boxes, zero rework.

I bring to client builds, consulting and partnerships the same functional-modeling discipline I created for complex systems: breaking large problems into small, clear, verifiable functions before writing unnecessary code.

  1. Root diagnosis

    Mapping the real business objective, how the end user actually behaves, and the exact bottleneck holding results back — before picking any tool.

  2. Explicit scope & decision

    Choosing the simplest, sturdiest path that solves the problem, with costs, trade-offs and out-of-scope items stated upfront so timelines stay honest.

  3. Modular, proven build

    Building piece by piece with real functional and security checks on every module. Nothing ships on guesswork.

  4. Autonomous handoff

    Shipping fast, clean and documented in production so you own your operation without being locked into artificial retainers.

Trajectory

Where this way of building comes from.

I started by going deep into two fields that rarely sit at the same table. On one side, financial markets and cryptoassets — which I have followed hands-on since 2012/2013 — alongside distributed systems and complex-systems architecture. On the other, the mechanics of human decision-making: I trained in clinical and conversational hypnosis, behavior analysis and scientific NLP, and studied Alfred Adler’s psychology, Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) and symbolic networks in depth.

That combination changed how I look at any operation. Sound technical builds fail when they ignore how people actually decide, and strong commercial theses break when they depend on heavy, fragile infrastructure.

Over the past three years, working remotely across Asia and other parts of the world, I turned that synthesis into live products of my own: I founded DLT Academy as a lean, AI-first ecosystem for financial decisions, published a book in two languages and architected Drayker. Today I bring that same end-to-end diagnosis, architecture and execution to companies, founders and partners.
